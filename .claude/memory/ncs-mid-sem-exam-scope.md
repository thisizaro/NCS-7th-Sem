Aranya is preparing for the **mid-semester exam** in Network and Cyber Security
(CS40017, 7th sem, KIIT). Per the 2026 lesson plan the mid-sem window is
**8-13 September**, and the delivery plan places the mid-sem break directly
after Unit II.

Mid-sem scope is therefore **Unit I (6 lectures) + Unit II (12 lectures) only**.
Unit II is by far the larger block and carries most of the weight.
Units III-V (Cyber Law, Authentication, Firewalls) are end-semester material.

Marks split: Internal 30 + Mid-sem 20 + End-sem 50.

See [[ncs-decks-syllabus-mismatch]] for why most of this scope has no slides.
