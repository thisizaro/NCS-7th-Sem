For NCS exam prep Aranya wants **all four** output formats: condensed unit notes,
practice Q&A with model answers, diagrams/comparison tables, and
flashcards/quick-recall.

Initially he said "only study what's here", then reversed it: for anything in the
**mid-sem** scope that the decks don't cover, **write it from my own knowledge or
research** rather than leaving it out.

**Why:** Unit II is 12 lectures with zero source material in the repo
([[ncs-decks-syllabus-mismatch]]), so restricting to the decks would leave most
of the exam uncovered.

**How to apply:** Organize `study/` by syllabus unit, never by deck number. Fill
gaps from TB1 (Stallings 8e) and TB2 (Whitman & Mattord 7e) terminology, since
those are the prescribed texts. Flag clearly in the notes which sections came
from outside the decks so he knows what to cross-check with his teacher.
